Foundations of Leadership & Skill

Military Training & Premier Academies

How raw recruits and officer cadets are forged into resilient, ethical, and intellectually capable military leaders through rigorous physical conditioning, tactical doctrine, and values.

The Guiding Officer Ethos

The Chetwode Creed

“The safety, honour and welfare of your country come first, always and every time.
The honour, welfare and comfort of the men you command come next.
Your own ease, comfort and safety come last, always and every time.”

Inscribed on the Chetwode Hall at the Indian Military Academy, Dehradun (1932)

Pre-Commissioning Training

Premier Officer Training Academies

Institutions responsible for transforming selected candidates into commissioned officers.

Tri-Services Joint Foundation

National Defence Academy (NDA)

Location: Khadakwasla, Pune, Maharashtra
Established: 1949 (Permanent campus dedicated 1954)
Mandate: The world's first tri-service academy, where Army, Navy, and Air Force cadets train together for three years, earning a university degree alongside military drill, weapon handling, horse riding, and fieldcraft before proceeding to service-specific academies.

Permanent Commission

Indian Military Academy (IMA)

Location: Dehradun, Uttarakhand
Established: 1 October 1932
Mandate: The cradle of officer leadership for the Indian Army. Trains Gentlemen Cadets from NDA, Direct Entry graduates, and Technical Graduates. Rigorous physical, tactical, and character development culminates in the famous Passing Out Parade and "Antim Pag" (Final Step).

Short Service Commission

Officers Training Academy (OTA)

Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu
Established: 15 January 1963
Mandate: Established originally during the 1962 emergency. Currently conducts a 49-week intensive training course for men and women officers selected for the Short Service Commission (SSC).

Lifelong Professional Development

The Three Stages of Military Training

Training in the Indian Army does not end at commissioning; it is an ongoing career pathway designed for escalating operational responsibilities.

Stage 1: Ab Initio

Basic Military Training

For soldiers, 36 to 44 weeks at Regimental Training Centres (e.g., Maratha Light Infantry Regimental Centre, Kumaon Regimental Centre). Teaches discipline, drill, marksmanship, field survival, and regiment history. For officers, 1 to 4 years at NDA/IMA/OTA.

Stage 2: Specialisation

Young Officers (YO) Courses

After commissioning, every officer attends their parent arm's school (e.g. Infantry School Mhow, School of Artillery Deolali, Armoured Corps Centre & School Ahmednagar) to master weapon systems, vehicle maintenance, and junior tactical leadership.

Stage 3: Strategic Command

Higher Staff & War Colleges

Senior mid-career officers attend the Defence Services Staff College (DSSC, Wellington) for staff appointments, followed by the Higher Command Course at Army War College (Mhow), and selected flag-rank officers attend the National Defence College (NDC, New Delhi).

Terrain-Specific Excellence

Specialised Warfare Training Institutions

Institutions recognized internationally for preparing soldiers to operate in extreme weather and asymmetric environments.

Sub-Zero Mountain Warfare

High Altitude Warfare School (HAWS)

Location: Gulmarg & Sonamarg, Jammu and Kashmir
Trains personnel in technical rock-climbing, avalanche safety, glacier survival, and winter ski-mobility. Essential preparation for troops deployed to Siachen Glacier and Ladakh peaks exceeding 15,000 feet.

Counter-Terrorism & Bushcraft

Counter Insurgency & Jungle Warfare School (CIJWS)

Location: Vairengte, Mizoram
Motto: "Fight the Guerrilla like a Guerrilla." Renowned worldwide for imparting specialized tactical training to Indian and friendly foreign militaries (including US, UK, and French armed forces) in counter-terrorism.

Technical Combat Engineering

College of Military Engineering (CME)

Location: Dapodi, Pune, Maharashtra
Premier technical training institute for the Corps of Engineers. Specialises in combat bridging across water obstacles, bomb disposal, de-mining, defensive construction, and CBRN (Chemical, Biological, Radiological, Nuclear) defence.
