Materiel & Capabilities

Equipment, Technology & Modernisation

An educational index of primary ground combat platforms, artillery systems, aviation assets, and indigenous technological initiatives under Atmanirbhar Bharat.

Modernisation Paradigm

Transition to Self-Reliance & Network-Centric Warfare

The Indian Army's materiel modernization operates under two complementary mandates: addressing immediate frontier operational requirements and establishing a robust indigenous defence industrial base through the Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat programs.

Procurements are increasingly channelled through Indian private and public sector manufacturers—including Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL), and Larsen & Toubro (L&T)—supplemented by joint-venture production agreements that transfer advanced manufacturing capabilities to Indian soil.

Armoured Combat · Operational

T-90S Bhishma MBT

Type: Main Battle Tank (3rd Generation)
Armament: 125mm 2A46M smoothbore gun, capable of firing guided anti-tank missiles (Invar), explosive reactive armour (Kontakt-5).
Role: Spearheads strike formations in desert and semi-desert warfare. Over 1,000 units in active service, assembled at Heavy Vehicles Factory (HVF) Avadi.

Armoured Combat · Operational / Mk-1A

Arjun Main Battle Tank

Type: Indigenous Heavy Main Battle Tank
Armament: 120mm rifled main gun with indigenously developed Kanchan composite armour.
Role: Developed by the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO). The upgraded Arjun Mk-1A incorporates 71 improvements including advanced thermal sights and mine ploughs.

Mechanised Infantry · Operational

BMP-2 Sarath

Type: Amphibious Tracked Infantry Combat Vehicle (ICV)
Armament: 30mm 2A42 dual-feed autocannon, Konkurs ATGM launcher.
Role: Transports infantry squads rapidly across combat zones with amphibious water-crossing capabilities; built under license by Ordnance Factory Medak.

Artillery · Operational

K9 Vajra-T 155mm / 52-Calibre

Type: Tracked Self-Propelled Howitzer
Range: Over 38 km with base-bleed projectiles.
Role: Manufactured in India by Larsen & Toubro (L&T) at Hazira under technology transfer from Hanwha Aerospace. Deployed effectively in western desert terrain and high-altitude Ladakh sectors.

Artillery · Operational

M777 Ultra-Light Howitzer

Type: 155mm / 39-Calibre Titanium Towed Howitzer
Range: Up to 30 km; weight ~4,200 kg.
Role: Designed for mountain deployment. Its lightweight titanium construction enables air-lifting by Indian Air Force CH-47 Chinook helicopters directly onto high Himalayan forward mountain ridges.

Artillery · Operational / Indigenous

Pinaka Multi-Barrel Rocket Launcher

Type: 214mm Multi-Barrel Artillery Rocket System (MBRL)
Range: 40 km (Mark-I) to 75 km (Guided Extended Range).
Role: Indigenous system developed by DRDO and manufactured by Tata Advanced Systems and L&T. Can salvo fire 12 high-explosive rockets in 44 seconds to saturate area targets.

Army Aviation · Operational / Indigenous

HAL Dhruv Advanced Light Helicopter

Type: Twin-Engine Multi-Role Utility Helicopter
Manufacturer: Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL)
Role: The workhorse of high-altitude logistics, search and rescue, casualty evacuation, and troop transport across Siachen Glacier, Ladakh, and Arunachal Pradesh.

Army Aviation · Induction Phase

HAL Prachand Light Combat Helicopter (LCH)

Type: Dedicated Combat Attack Helicopter
Role: World's only attack helicopter specifically designed to take off and land at altitudes of 5,000 meters with full weapons payload. Features stealth fuselage, 20mm nose turret cannon, and air-to-air Mistral missiles.

Infantry · Operational / Production

Infantry Small Arms Modernisation

Standard Assault Rifles: SIG Sauer 716i (7.62x51mm calibre) deployed with frontline troops; Kalashnikov AK-203 (7.62x39mm calibre) manufactured indigenously by Indo-Russia Rifles Private Limited at Korwa, Amethi.
Sniper Rifles: Sako TRG-42 and Beretta .338 Lapua Magnum for designated mountain sharpshooters.

Surveillance & EW · Operational

BEL Battlefield Surveillance Radar (BFSR-SR)

Type: Man-Portable Short-Range Doppler Radar
Role: Developed by DRDO and manufactured by Bharat Electronics Limited. Detects walking troops, vehicles, and infiltrators day and night in dense vegetation or fog along the Line of Control.
