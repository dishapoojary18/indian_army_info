Project Statement & Institutional Independence

“Indian Army — A Nation's Shield” is an independent academic educational project developed to present an accessible, accurate, and objective study of the Indian Army's history, doctrine, command structure, and humanitarian service for educational presentation and viva examination.

Disclaimer: This website is NOT an official portal of the Indian Army, the Ministry of Defence, or the Government of India. It does not solicit recruitments, process applications, or speak on behalf of the armed forces. For official recruitment or official service notifications, citizens must consult the official portal at indianarmy.nic.in or joinindianarmy.nic.in.

Terminology Reference

Military Terminology Glossary

Command

The highest territorial and operational formation in the Indian Army. Commanded by a three-star Lieutenant General (GOC-in-C), overseeing field Corps within a defined geographical theatre. India maintains 6 operational commands and 1 training command (ARTRAC).

Corps

A major tactical field formation comprising 2 to 3 divisions plus dedicated artillery and engineer brigades (60,000–85,000 troops). Commanded by a Lieutenant General (GOC). Classified as "Strike Corps" (offensive) or "Holding Corps" (defensive).

Division

The primary self-contained combined-arms formation capable of independent sustained operations (15,000–20,000 troops). Commanded by a Major General. Classified into Mountain, Infantry, Armoured, or RAPID Divisions.

Brigade

A tactical formation consisting of 3 to 4 fighting battalions or regiments, supported by combat engineers, artillery, and medical assets (approx. 3,000–4,500 troops). Commanded by a Brigadier.

Battalion / Regiment

The basic operational and tactical unit of ground warfare. In the Infantry, it is styled as a Battalion (~850–1,000 soldiers commanded by a Colonel). In the Armoured Corps and Artillery, the equivalent unit is styled as a Regiment.

Junior Commissioned Officer (JCO)

A unique personnel tier holding a gazetted presidential commission, ranking between Commissioned Officers and Other Ranks. Includes Naib Subedar, Subedar, and Subedar Major. Serves as the critical operational bridge between command and enlisted jawans.

Line of Control (LoC)

The military control line separating Indian and Pakistani-controlled portions of Jammu and Kashmir, delineated originally as the Ceasefire Line (CFL) in 1949 and mutually formalized under the Simla Agreement on 2 July 1972.

Line of Actual Control (LAC)

The effective border demarcation separating Indian-controlled territory from Chinese-controlled territory across three sectors: Western (Ladakh), Middle (Uttarakhand/Himachal), and Eastern (Arunachal Pradesh/Sikkim).

HADR

Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief: military operations conducted to relieve civilian suffering caused by natural catastrophes (floods, earthquakes, tsunamis) or man-made disasters in coordination with national and civil disaster management agencies.

Permanent Commission (PC)

A full military career commission entitling an officer to serve until the age of retirement according to rank, with eligibility for senior command appointments and full statutory pension benefits.
